Number Bonds to 20

🔄

Commutative Law

Order doesn't matter in adding! 3+5 = 5+3 = 8! You can swap the numbers! 🔄

✖️ Multiplication!

✖️

What is Multiplication?

3 × 4 means "3 groups of 4"! It's faster than adding 4+4+4! The answer is called the product!

🔢

Times Tables

Learning your times tables helps you multiply quickly! 2×, 5×, and 10× are the easiest to learn first!

🔄

Multiplication is Commutative

3×4 = 4×3 = 12! You can swap the numbers and get the same answer! Cool, right? 😎

🌟

Multiply by 0 and 1

Anything × 0 = 0! (zero groups = nothing!) Anything × 1 = itself! (one group = the number!)

🍪

Arrays

An array shows multiplication! 3 rows of 4 cookies 🍪 = 12 cookies. Rows × Columns = Total!

📈

Skip Counting = Multiplying!

Counting by 5s: 5, 10, 15, 20… is the same as your 5 times table! You already know it! 🎉

🔷 Shapes All Around!

🔷
📐

2D Shapes (Flat)

2D shapes are flat like paper! Circle, square, triangle, rectangle – they have no thickness!

📦

3D Shapes (Solid)

3D shapes are solid! Cube (dice 🎲), sphere (ball ⚽), cylinder (tin can 🥫) – you can hold them!

📏

Sides & Corners

Shapes have sides (edges) and corners (vertices)! A triangle has 3 of each!

🦋

Symmetry

A shape is symmetrical when both halves match after folding! Like a butterfly! 🦋

🔺

Angles

An angle is the space between two sides meeting! Right angle = a perfect square corner! Like the corner of this screen!

🌍

Shapes in Real Life

Pizza = circle 🍕, door = rectangle, sandwich = triangle 🥪, dice = cube, ball = sphere ⚽!

📏 Measuring Things!

How long? How heavy? How much? Measuring tells us!

📏
📏

Length

We measure in mm, cm, and m! 10mm = 1cm. 100cm = 1m. A door is about 2m tall!

⚖️

Weight / Mass

We measure in g and kg! 1000g = 1kg. A bag of sugar is 1kg. A pencil is about 5g!

🧃

Capacity

How much fits inside? We use ml and litres! 1000ml = 1 litre. A big bottle is 1.5 litres!

🌡️

Temperature

Measured in °C! Water boils at 100°C. Ice forms at 0°C. Your body is ~37°C!

📐

Area

Area = how much surface a shape covers! Count the squares inside a shape to find the area! 📐

🔄

Perimeter

Perimeter = the distance all the way around a shape! Add up all the sides! Like a fence around a garden! 🌻

Grouping

Fairness
