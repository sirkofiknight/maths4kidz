➕➖ Adding & Subtracting

➕

Addition

Adding makes numbers BIGGER! 2 apples + 3 apples = 5 apples 🍎🍎🍎🍎🍎

Subtraction

Taking away makes numbers SMALLER! 5 cookies – 2 = 3 cookies 🍪🍪🍪

Equals Sign (=)

The equals sign means "the same as"! 3 + 2 = 5 is the same as 5! Both sides balance! ⚖️

Number Bonds to 10

3+7=10, 6+4=10, 5+5=10, 8+2=10, 9+1=10! Learn them all by heart! 💪

🔢

Number Bonds to 20

12+8=20, 15+5=20, 11+9=20! Bonds help you add fast in your head! 🧠

🔄

Commutative Law

Order doesn't matter in adding! 3+5 = 5+3 = 8! You can swap the numbers! 🔄

✖️

What is Multiplication?

3 × 4 means "3 groups of 4"! It's faster than adding 4+4+4! The answer is called the product!

🔢

Times Tables

Learning your times tables helps you multiply quickly! 2×, 5×, and 10× are the easiest to learn first!

🔄

Multiplication is Commutative

3×4 = 4×3 = 12! You can swap the numbers and get the same answer! Cool, right? 😎

🌟

Multiply by 0 and 1

Anything × 0 = 0! (zero groups = nothing!) Anything × 1 = itself! (one group = the number!)

🍪

Arrays

An array shows multiplication! 3 rows of 4 cookies 🍪 = 12 cookies. Rows × Columns = Total!

📈

Skip Counting = Multiplying!

Counting by 5s: 5, 10, 15, 20… is the same as your 5 times table! You already know it! 🎉

🔷 Shapes All Around!

Shapes are everywhere – spot them in real life!

🔷
📐

2D Shapes (Flat)

2D shapes are flat like paper! Circle, square, triangle, rectangle – they have no thickness!

📦

3D Shapes (Solid)

3D shapes are solid! Cube (dice 🎲), sphere (ball ⚽), cylinder (tin can 🥫) – you can hold them!

📏

Sides & Corners

Shapes have sides (edges) and corners (vertices)! A triangle has 3 of each!

🦋

Symmetry

A shape is symmetrical when both halves match after folding! Like a butterfly! 🦋

🔺

Angles

An angle is the space between two sides meeting! Right angle = a perfect square corner! Like the corner of this screen!

🌍

Shapes in Real Life

Pizza = circle 🍕, door = rectangle, sandwich = triangle 🥪, dice = cube, ball = sphere ⚽!

🍕 Fractions!

Sharing equally makes fractions – fair is fun!

🍕
½

One Half (½)

Cut something into 2 EQUAL parts. Each part is ONE HALF! Like sharing a sandwich! 🥪

¼

One Quarter (¼)

Cut into 4 EQUAL parts. Each is ONE QUARTER! Sharing pizza with 3 friends! 🍕

⅓

One Third (⅓)

Cut into 3 EQUAL parts. Each is ONE THIRD! 3 friends share chocolate! 🍫

🎯

The Whole (1)

When nothing is cut = the WHOLE! 2 halves = 1 whole. 4 quarters = 1 whole!

📊

Comparing Fractions

½ is BIGGER than ¼! More equal pieces = smaller each piece! Imagine cutting a cake more and more ways!

🔢

Numerator & Denominator

In ¾: the 3 (top) = how many pieces you have. The 4 (bottom) = how many equal pieces total!

📏 Measuring Things!

How long? How heavy? How much? Measuring tells us!

📏
📏

Length

We measure in mm, cm, and m! 10mm = 1cm. 100cm = 1m. A door is about 2m tall!

⚖️

Weight / Mass

We measure in g and kg! 1000g = 1kg. A bag of sugar is 1kg. A pencil is about 5g!

🧃

Capacity

How much fits inside? We use ml and litres! 1000ml = 1 litre. A big bottle is 1.5 litres!

🌡️

Temperature

Measured in °C! Water boils at 100°C. Ice forms at 0°C. Your body is ~37°C!

📐

Area

Area = how much surface a shape covers! Count the squares inside a shape to find the area! 📐

🔄

Perimeter

Perimeter = the distance all the way around a shape! Add up all the sides! Like a fence around a garden! 🌻

Equal Sharing

Division means splitting a group into EQUAL smaller groups! Everyone gets the same amount!

Division Symbol (÷)

This symbol ÷ means "divided by". It's like the opposite of multiplication!

½

Division & Halves

Dividing by 2 is the same as finding HALF! 10 ÷ 2 = 5. Half of 10 is 5!

🎾

Grouping

You can divide by putting things into groups. 12 balls into groups of 3 = 4 groups!

⚖️

Fairness

Maths is fair! In division, every group MUST have the same number of items.
